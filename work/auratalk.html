01

Overview

A web application designed to help people with Aphasia practice speaking anytime, anywhere, using AI-powered speech recognition to support their exercises.

Built with a team — M18: The Procrastinators — as a finalist entry for HIT Award 2026 (Health Innovation and Technology Award). The project was also a semifinalist at NSC 2025 and received an Honorable Award at the AI Thailand Hackathon 2024.

02

Features

  • Two user roles — SLP (Speech-Language Pathologist) and Patient
  • SLPs can manage patients, assign lessons, and track progress
  • Patients complete speaking exercises with AI-powered speech recognition
  • Thai speech-to-text powered by Whisper
  • Text-to-speech support via the VAJA API
  • Secure authentication with hashed passwords
